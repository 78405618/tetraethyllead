Tetraethyl Lead TEL-B / TEL-CB Specifications and Transport Data

This page summarizes the specifications and transport classification of the tetraethyl lead (TEL, CAS 78-00-2) blends TEL-B and TEL-CB sold by Nanchang Haixu Industrial Co., Ltd. It is for qualified industrial customers only; the batch COA, technical data sheet (TDS) and safety data sheet (SDS) prevail.

Important: most countries and regions have banned lead additives in motor gasoline. We supply TEL-CB only where importing-country law permits it, the customer holds the importing-country import permit (import letter) and passes verification; the customer must ensure the use is lawful. TEL is a strictly regulated, highly toxic hazardous chemical; applicable laws, regulations and the official SDS prevail.
