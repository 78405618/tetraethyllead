Tetraethyl Lead (TEL) FAQ

Frequently asked questions on tetraethyl lead (TEL-B) technical consultation, TDS/SDS interpretation, UN 1649 transport and purchase conditions.

What is tetraethyl lead (TEL)?

Tetraethyl lead (CAS 78-00-2) is an organolead compound historically used as a gasoline anti-knock agent. It is a strictly controlled hazardous chemical and now exists mainly in blends such as TEL-B for specific industrial fields.

Which grades do your technical services cover?

Technical document interpretation typically covers TEL-B blend (TEL ≥61.48% wt, blue transparent liquid) and TEL-CB motor gasoline grade blend. Specific figures are subject to the TDS.

Can you help interpret TDS and SDS documents?

Yes. For qualified industrial customers, we assist in interpreting key specifications, safety information and storage/transport requirements in the TDS and SDS.

What support is available for dangerous goods documents?

TEL-B is a UN 1649 Class 6.1 dangerous good. We provide technical communication and coordination support on transport document requirements, subject to applicable laws and carrier rules.

Do you sell tetraethyl lead (TEL)?

Yes. We sell only to qualified industrial customers in lawful jurisdictions that hold the importing country import permit (import letter). Products are supplied by a manufacturer holding all production and related permits. We do not sell to individuals or unauthorized uses; pricing and supply follow qualification and end-use verification.

What are the handling and storage requirements?

This is a highly toxic hazardous chemical. Use closed systems and strict protection, avoiding skin contact and inhalation. Store locked, sealed and away from ignition sources and oxidizers, managed only by authorized professionals. The official SDS prevails.
